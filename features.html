Feature map

What's in Rootweave

Current capabilities. Not a roadmap.

Home Patch

Compost → wait → plant

🪣 Compost wakes dead ground. Wait for the bed. Plant 🌿 Pioneers or 🌽 Three Sisters.

Inspect + soil health

👆 Inspect a tile for the soil story. Home Patch has the soil-health overlay.

Readable tiles

Dead, sleeping, awake, and pond beds read differently. Sleeping ground shows ready in X days.

One Play click

▶️ Play advances exactly one labeled day. Not an idle loop.

Journey

Valley map

One drawing of the valley — house, laneway, and river stay on the map.

Tap a fold

Tap an unlocked fold and you walk that land. Locked neighbors show the real unlock line.

Neighbors after Home

After Home Patch, leftover and neighbor folds open as you heal the next fields.

Animals & water

Cattle & chickens

Back Paddock brings cattle, then chickens. Move the herd so the pad can rest.

Ponds & streams

Later plots have pond and stream tiles. Creek Bend teaches buffers on the banks.

Coins

Earned, not bought

Coins come from harvest and the neighbor stand (after Home). Paid coin packs are not sold.

Valley Shop

River Reach opens the Valley Shop. It spends earned coins on land tools — not a pay-to-win checkout.

Free & saves

Play is free

No ads. No paid coin packs. Play Billing is not in this build. Web and Play closed-test TWA (?store=play) share this path.

Optional support

A tip link lives under About. It does not grant coins or unlocks.

Export / Import / New Game

Download a JSON backup from About. Import it later. New Game wipes the valley.

Homestead & freeplay

Homestead unlocks after Home Patch. Valley freeplay opens when the campaign is cleared.

Named limits

What this is not

Not a pay-to-win shop. No ads. No Play Billing. Home Patch is the start — some folds stay locked until you heal neighbors. Optional support is a tip only; it does not add coins.

This is the farm that exists. Not the farm we wish we had.
