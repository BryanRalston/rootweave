Privacy Policy

Effective: August 13, 2026 · Product: Rootweave

Short version: Rootweave is an offline-first farm simulator. Your save lives in this browser’s localStorage. There is no account and no backend collecting farm data.

1. Who we are

Rootweave is a regenerative farm sim published by Bryan Ralston.

Live game: https://bryanralston.github.io/rootweave/

Contact: use the support email on the Google Play listing once you set it. Placeholder until then: add your address in Play Console (this page will be updated when you publish a public contact).

2. Data stored on your device

The game stores data locally (browser localStorage), including:

  • Campaign / plot progress and homestead layout
  • In-game coins, unlocks, and settings (mute, tips seen)
  • Optional JSON backups you export yourself

This information is used only to run the game on your device. It is not uploaded by the app.

3. What we do not collect

  • No account, login, or cloud save
  • No advertising ID, analytics SDK, or crash reporter shipped with the game
  • No backend that receives farm data
  • No sale of personal information

4. Network use

When you play over HTTPS, the browser may fetch the page, sprites, and a service-worker update. That is ordinary static-file hosting (GitHub Pages). Game saves are not sent with those requests.

If you install from Google Play via a Trusted Web Activity, Google Play services may process standard install and update data under Google’s policies.

5. Exports you create

Export save writes a JSON file on your device. You choose whether to share or keep it.

6. Children

Rootweave is a calm educational / simulation game, but it is not directed at children under 13. Play IARC / content rating is completed by the publisher in Play Console.

7. Retention & deletion

Data stays on the device until you use New Game, clear site data, or uninstall. Uninstalling or clearing browser storage deletes the local farm.

8. Security

Because the save is local, protect the device. Shared computers will share the same browser profile’s farm.

9. Changes

The effective date at the top changes if this policy changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: publisher Bryan Ralston, via the support email on the store listing. Fill that field before production launch.
